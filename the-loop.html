WHAT IS THE LOOP?

The Loop is a dry, platform-studded world where nothing moves unless a cadet tells it to — in exactly the order they said it. This is where Rover-7 first got stranded, and where every platform since has proven the same thing: a computer is honest to a fault, and being precise is a skill, not a personality trait. Sequence, loops, nested loops, events, conditions, debugging — ten platforms, one skill at a time, building to Milestone Test 1, the first time everything learned has to hold up combined.
