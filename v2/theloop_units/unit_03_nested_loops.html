📖 Field Manual: NESTED LOOPS

A NESTED LOOP is a REPEAT block placed inside another REPEAT block. Three rules to remember:

  1. The inner loop always finishes completely before the outer loop moves to its next repeat — that's what lets one full row of a sweep finish before the next row starts.
  2. Order still matters at both levels — inside the inner loop, and among everything inside the outer loop (including where the inner loop itself sits).
  3. Think of it like a weekly routine: the outer loop is "do this for every row of the sweep," and the inner loop is the steps that sweep across just one row.
