📖 Field Manual: REPEAT

A REPEAT block runs the same steps multiple times without you writing them out each time. Three rules to remember:

  1. Everything inside the loop happens every single time it repeats.
  2. Order inside the loop still matters — just like a regular sequence. A loop repeats your steps exactly as written; it doesn't fix a wrong order.
  3. A loop doesn't just save typing — it lets one instruction stand for many. That's why Command Control limits your program slots.

Worked example. Say the route is 9 straight moves Up in a row. Written out plain, that's 9 program slots — way over most budgets. Written as a loop, it's just one:

🔁 REPEAT × 9 [ ⬆ Up ]

Same 9 moves, same result — but it only costs 1 slot on the SLOTS chip instead of 9.
