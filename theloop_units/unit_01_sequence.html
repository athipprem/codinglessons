📖 Field Manual: SEQUENCE

A list of exact steps, done in a specific order, is called a sequence. Two rules to remember:

  1. Order matters. Swap two steps, get a different — or broken — result.
  2. Nothing is assumed. If you didn't command it, it didn't happen.
