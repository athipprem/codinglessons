📖 Field Manual: EVENTS

An EVENT block only runs its steps when its trigger actually happens. Three rules to remember:

  1. An event fires when the world decides, not when your program gets there. You can't schedule it — you can be ready for it.
  2. You don't control WHEN or WHERE it fires — the mission does. You only decide WHAT happens when it does.
  3. It can go anywhere in your program — it isn't a step in your sequence. It's "listening" the whole time the mission runs, no matter where you place it in your program list.

Worked example — the handler can sit first, last, or in the middle of your program; it still fires the instant its trigger happens, not when your program "reaches" it:

🔔 On Signal Beacon [ ➡ Right, ➡ Right ]
🔁 REPEAT × 3 [ ⬆ Up ]

Even placed first, that handler does nothing until Rover-7 actually crosses the beacon partway through the REPEAT below it — then it fires right then, out of order, exactly when the trigger happens.
