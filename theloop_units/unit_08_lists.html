📖 Field Manual: LISTS

Three rules to remember:

  1. A list holds many values under one name, in order — like MANIFEST: [RED, BLUE, GREEN].
  2. Position matters: the first item gets delivered first, the second item second, and so on.
  3. You work through a list front to back, one item at a time — the list says what comes next, even when something else is closer.
